I’m a field geologist, born and raised in Taiwan, passionate about the sediments, surface processes, and geological mechanisms that drive topographic evolution in mountains (erosional “source” of sediments) and form stratigraphy in sedimentary basins (depositional “sink” for sediments). When I’m not immersed in research, I enjoy hiking, playing basketball, and snowboarding.

I work at the Quantitative Clastics Laboratory, an industry-supported research consortium at the Bureau of Economic Geology.

Research

East Taiwan basins

Sedimentary basins, mélange, and crustal deformation at the active collisional suture of eastern Taiwan.

Landslides

Deep-seated landslides, their controls and triggers, and their long-term effects on topography.

Bedrock channels

How lithology and sediment set channel slope and form in rapidly eroding, landslide-prone terrain.

Quantitative stratigraphy

Data-science methods for correlation, facies classification, and sedimentological signal processing.

Source-to-sink modeling

Linking geomorphic signals to stratigraphic archives with numerical source-to-sink simulation.

Gravel beaches

Sedimentologic and morphologic characteristics of gravel-rich beaches. Details to be updated.
