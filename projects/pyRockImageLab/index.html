pyRockImageLab

  • PyPI

pyRockImageLab (pyrockimagelab) is for rock-photograph workflows. The work underway is core-box crop, stitch, and mosaic. Later room is planned for hand samples, outcrop photos, and machine-learning identification.

The name is reserved on PyPI as version 0.0.1. It is under development and not ready for use. A fuller release is on the way. The public listing uses the GNU Affero General Public License 3.0 or later, and it targets Python 3.9 or newer.
